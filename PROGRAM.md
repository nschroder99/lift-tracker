# Upper / Lower A-B — 2 on, 1 off

```
Day 1  Upper A      Day 4  Upper B
Day 2  Lower A      Day 5  Lower B
Day 3  rest         Day 6  rest
```

Every muscle is trained twice per 6-day cycle (~2.3×/week). Weekly hard sets
land at 12–15 for every major muscle — inside the 10–20 band where the
dose-response data flattens.

## Progression

**Double progression.** Stay at a weight until you hit the *top* of the rep
range on *every* set at or under the target RIR. Then add the smallest jump
(5 lb upper, 10 lb lower) and rebuild to the top of the range. The app detects
this and tells you when to go up.

## Deload

Every 6 cycles (~8 weeks): run one cycle at **half the sets, same weights**.

## Nutrition

You will not get visibly bigger without this part.

- **Surplus:** ~300–400 kcal over maintenance. Target **0.25–0.5% of bodyweight
  gained per week**. Faster than that is mostly fat.
- **Protein:** **1.6–2.2 g/kg** (0.7–1.0 g/lb). Returns flatten above ~1.6 g/kg —
  treat it as a floor, not a target to chase.
- **Sleep:** 7–9 h. Short sleep measurably blunts the response to training.
